# Capital Accounts Module

**Status: Placeholder for Future Development**

This module will handle equity transactions that require special accounting treatment.

## Planned Features

### Stock Transactions
- Common stock issuances
- Preferred stock issuances
- Stock repurchases (treasury stock)
- Stock splits

### Capital Contributions
- PE capital contributions
- Partner capital calls
- Owner draws

### Dividends
- Cash dividends declared
- Stock dividends
- Dividend payments

### Other Equity
- Retained earnings adjustments
- OCI reclassifications
- Prior period adjustments
